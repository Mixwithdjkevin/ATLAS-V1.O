ACADEMY QUESTIONS

Do I need prior musical experience to join?

No prior musical background is required. We start from ground zero: understanding music bar structures, BPM counting, and equipment fundamentals.

What is the difference between Online and Offline?

Online courses are live 1-on-1 video sessions + HD student portal suitable for worldwide students. Offline courses take place physically at DJ Kevin's Mumbai studio on CDJ-3000 setups.

Yes! All graduates receive an official Musik N Beatz Certified Pro DJ Certificate along with a recorded 60-minute promo mix.
