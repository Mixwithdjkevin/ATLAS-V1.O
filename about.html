ABOUT DJ KEVIN

With over 10+ years of active nightclub residencies, festival headlining slots, and high-end wedding gigs, DJ Kevin is recognized as one of the most technical and versatile DJs in the region.

Frustrated by the low quality, unorganized, and clipped audio packs floating around online, Kevin created Mix With DJ Kevin — a dedicated ecosystem providing DJs with clean, gain-normalized, Rekordbox & Serato pre-cued music collections and hands-on academy training.
